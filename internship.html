GeoAI & Data Science Internship

GEBA Internship Program

GEBA Internship Program is a practical 8–12 week learning experience offered during the July–October period for high school and university students interested in GeoAI, data science, remote sensing, coding, environmental intelligence, and real-world project development.

Internship Program

From curiosity to real-world GeoAI projects

The GEBA internship introduces students to data thinking, coding practice, GeoAI concepts, remote sensing applications, and team-based project development through a 12-week learning structure. The program is designed to make data science approachable by connecting everyday questions, environmental observations, and practical coding exercises.

Internship History

Activities, learning moments, project progress

Internship journey with weekly photos, short descriptions, student learning activities, and project milestones.

Week 1 · Virtual Orientation

The internship began with a virtual orientation introducing the program structure, learning goals, and 8–12 week roadmap. Students were introduced to data science, GeoAI, Earth observation, and the team project they will develop throughout the program.

Week 2 · Data Science & Data Analysis

Students learned the fundamentals of data science and how raw data can be organized, explored, and interpreted to answer real-world questions. Hands-on activities introduced basic data analysis workflows and encouraged students to identify patterns, variables, and relationships in data.

Week 3 · Data Visualization Practice

Students practiced transforming data into visual information using charts and graphical displays. The activities focused on understanding how visualization can reveal trends, comparisons, and patterns that may be difficult to recognize from raw numbers alone.

Week 4 · Prediction Modeling

Students were introduced to predictive modeling and how data can be used to estimate unknown or future outcomes. They practiced connecting input variables with model results while learning the importance of interpretation, accuracy, and model evaluation.

Week 5 · Earth Observation

Students explored Earth observation and learned how satellites and remote sensing provide information about the Earth's surface and environment. They examined spatial imagery and discussed how environmental conditions can be observed and analyzed from space.

Week 6 · Google Earth Engine

Students gained hands-on experience with Google Earth Engine for exploring satellite imagery and geospatial data. They practiced navigating locations, examining Earth observation datasets, and connecting cloud-based mapping tools with GeoAI and environmental applications.
